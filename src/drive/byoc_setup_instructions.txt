Bring Your Own Credentials - Setup Instructions
===============================================

If you find that you keep hitting rate limits with rclone or would prefer
higher download speeds, you can create your own Google OAuth credentials to
use with Synchotic! This takes about ten minutes to set up in a browser but
guarantees you get your own drive quota at full speeds rather than sharing it
with every other rclone user.

Anywhere below that gives you a name to type, the name does not matter to
Google. Type exactly what is written and you cannot get it wrong.

Instructions:

1. MAKE A PROJECT
   Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in.
   Project dropdown at the top, then "New Project".
     Project name:  synchotic-byoc
     Location:      leave whatever it already says
   Click Create, wait a few seconds, then pick synchotic-byoc in that same
   dropdown at the top. Everything after this happens inside that project.

2. TURN ON GOOGLE DRIVE
   Left menu: "APIs and Services", then "Library".
   Search "Google Drive API". Open it, click Enable.

3. FILL IN THE APP INFO
   Left menu: "APIs and Services", then "OAuth consent screen".
   Google now calls this page "Google Auth Platform". Click "Get started".

   App Information
     App name:            Synchotic
     User support email:  pick your own address from the dropdown
   Audience
     select External
   Contact Information
     Email addresses:     your own address again
   Finish
     tick the box agreeing to the user data policy, then Create.

4. SAY WHAT YOU ARE ASKING FOR
   Left menu of that same page: "Data Access", then "Add or remove scopes".
   In the filter box paste:

     https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly

   Tick it, click Update, then Save.
   Google will call this a restricted scope and warn you. That is expected.
   Nothing is being sent anywhere, the app runs on your own machine.

5. PUBLISH IT (DO NOT SKIP)
   Left menu: "Audience". Under "Publishing status" click "Publish app"
   and confirm.
   It must end up saying "In production". If it says "Testing", Google
   throws away your sign-in every 7 days and Synchotic keeps logging you out.

   If Google refuses and says your configuration is incomplete, go to
   "Branding" in that same left menu and fill in whatever it has flagged:
     App name:                   Synchotic
     User support email:         your own address
     App logo:                   leave empty
     Application home page:      https://github.com/noahbaxter/synchotic
     Privacy policy link:        https://noahbaxter.dev/synchotic/privacy.html
     Terms of service link:      leave empty unless it insists, then use
                                 https://github.com/noahbaxter/synchotic
     Authorized domains:         github.com
                                 noahbaxter.dev
     Developer contact:          your own address
   Save, then go back to "Audience" and publish again.

   You do NOT need Google to verify the app. Unverified is fine. The only
   thing that costs you is one scary screen the first time you sign in.

6. MAKE THE KEY
   Left menu: "Clients", then "Create client".
     Application type:  Desktop app
     Name:              Synchotic desktop
   Click Create.

7. DOWNLOAD IT
   In the box that pops up, click "Download JSON". If you closed the box,
   click your client in the list and use the download button at the top.

8. INSTALL IT
   Rename the file you just downloaded to "credentials.json" exactly.
   Move it into this folder, right next to these instructions.

9. ENABLE IT
   Start Synchotic, press D, choose "Use your own Google credentials", and
   sign in with the same Google account.
   Google will say the app is not verified. It is yours. Click "Advanced",
   then "Go to ... (unsafe)".


FAQ
---
Why am I being signed out every 7 days?
   Step 5 did not stick. Open "Audience" again. If publishing status still
   says "Testing", set it to "In production" and sign in again.

Why does Synchotic say it is not set up?
   The file must be named exactly "credentials.json", not "credentials(1).json",
   and must sit in this folder, beside these instructions.

The menu names do not match what I see.
   Google renames these pages every few months. "OAuth consent screen",
   "Google Auth Platform", "Branding", "Audience", "Clients" and "Data Access"
   are all the same area. Look for the words in bold above, not the exact path.
